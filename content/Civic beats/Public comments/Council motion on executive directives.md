> [!notice] Read the motion via the [City's LMS](https://lms.lacity.gov/files/26-0675).

I urge the Council to reject or table this motion as written. Any proposal to alter the balance of powers between the Council and Mayor, let alone one that directly contradicts a key recommendation of the Charter Reform Commission to expand mayoral powers, warrants further study, discussion, and consideration by an independent body like the Commission.

It is worth noting that this motion was initially received in May. Waiving it out of Committee now, potentially circumventing the will of the people entrusted to consider the impact of these changes, and placing it on the Council's agenda during the short, one-month window between our citywide elections and the inauguration of the Mayor-elect and new Councilmembers is ill-timed at best, and, frankly, a last-minute power grab at worst.

The City Council should not be unilaterally considering a motion to expand its own power over the incoming Mayor, let alone any action that would modify the Charter, during a lame-duck period. The voters have spoken, and the Council should allow the leaders they just elected to weigh in on a matter as critical to how our City functions as the separation of powers. Pushing this forward now risks undermining all the progress this body has made on reforming our Charter thus far.
