# **Local Propositions**

> [!danger]- **Prop TE, ULA tax exemption:** NO <code class="red">Endorsed</code>
>**At first glance, Prop TE appears to have good intentions:** grant temporary reprieve from the Measure ULA "mansion tax" (transfer tax) to homeowners in the Pacific Palisades affected by the devastating wildfires that ravaged the community last year.
> 
>**But this exemption leaves room for exploitation by the most wealthy,** those who own the so-called 'mansions' this measure was intended to target in the first place. I still believe Measure ULA's provisions need reform to allow new multi-family development to break even. Prop TE would reduce Measure ULA revenue by millions every year, taking from a now-critical funding source for affordable housing in the City and posing a challenge to future attempts at reform. Its passage would put the City in a lose-lose situation with Measure ULA in the future.
>
>**Vote NO on Prop TE.**
