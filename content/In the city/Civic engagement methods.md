# Senior research project

## Practices

* **Participatory budgeting**: Process involving groups of members of the public able to identify community priorities that budget should reflect ([Gilman, 2016](https://www.jstor.org/stable/j.ctt1c6v8hr.8))
* **Mini-publics:** Representative commissions of members of the public who are tasked with discussing and engaging on specific issues ([Hemingway & Pek, 2023](https://www.jstor.org/stable/10.2307/48749305))
