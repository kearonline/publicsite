Welcome to *The Kearlog*, my digital notebook.

I'm a Los Angeles-based recent urban planning graduate, relentless optimist, and jack of some trades who loves to dabble in a little bit of everything in my free time: from community organizing to graphic design. Above all, I'm just here to have fun.

I started this site as a running log of the kinds of policy, cultural, and societal whims that put me on research deep dives; the work I've done that deserves an online home; and the tools I make that I believe will help in advancing three core principles: **community, care, and accountability**. 

> [!notice] Everything here should be seen as being in draft form, always subject to change, and my own. Take all of this with a grain of salt, and keep in mind that I don't represent anyone other than myself.
