# Local Propositions

> [!danger]- Prop TE, ULA tax exemption: NO <code class="red">Endorsed</code>
>**At first glance, Prop TE appears to have good intentions:** grant temporary reprieve from the Measure ULA "mansion tax" (transfer tax) to homeowners in the Pacific Palisades affected by the devastating wildfires that ravaged the community last year. Palisadeans deserve relief, but a better approach is necessary to ensure affordable housing development from Measure ULA funding remains on track in the rest of the City.
>
>Prop TE's passage would weaken Measure ULA's provisions significantly by reducing revenue by millions per year, acting as a significant diversion of wealth from top earners away from affordable housing development as was initially intended. I still believe that Measure ULA is in desperate need of reform to allow new multi-family development to break even, as many have sounded the alarm about its unintended consequences since its passage in 2022. A significant reduction in revenue would pose a serious challenge to future attempts at reform that would further reduce it. 
>
>**Vote NO on Prop TE.**
