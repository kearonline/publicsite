> [!notice] Like everything else in this folder, this is fictional and written for a Minecraft server's roleplay government.

We are committed to leading an administration that will work with and for the citizens of Redmont in pursuit of a greater future for all. These seven goals for a better Redmont reflect our desire to deliver a dynamic platform that includes both common-sense reforms and sweeping changes to commonwealth policy.

# Make our government more open and accountable to the people.

The Redmont government, following the divisive events of the past few months, has faced a crisis of confidence from citizens across the political spectrum. We will work tirelessly to restore the public's trust in the government by always being honest with the people we serve and enacting the following policies to improve transparency and make our administration accountable to its constituents.

## Transparency

- Issue weekly press releases on the accomplishments of the administration.
- Conduct more open Cabinet meetings and release meeting notes and summaries on a regular basis.
- Open the Cabinet Discord server and use it for the release of information to the public more consistently.
- Identify new ways to announce and release election results that gets players involved in the process while maintaining election integrity. (DOS)

## Accountability

- All department officials and employees will be required to abide by a uniform minimum activity policy, to be developed in collaboration with the Cabinet.
- The president and vice president will host regular office hours where they can meet with members of the public to discuss pressing issues.
- Implement a background check process for high-clearance government occupations, including electoral officer, auditor, and prosecutor. (DOS, DLA)

## Structural Reform

- Department heads will be required to establish formal lines of succession for their respective agencies in the event of a vacancy.
- Reevaluate political party application requirements to ensure parties of all sizes are given a fair shot. (DOS)
- Encourage a greater use of rubrics and set criteria for evaluating applications of all kinds to limit bias as much as possible.
- Develop specific criteria for company activity checks from the Department of Commerce's compliance team. (DOC)

# Usher in a new era where open collaboration is the norm, not the exception.

Political partisanship and dirty politics are what we believe to be the most common barrier to getting things done for the Redmont people. We will strive to work across the aisle, with the Redmont people and our political opponents and allies alike to deliver change the only way we have known how, because we know that changes in political culture have to start at the very top. We aim to allow more opportunities for everyone to get involved in government.

## Citizen Involvement

- Host regular office hours for the president and vice president for meetings with members of the public.
- Conduct regular push-polls to gauge public opinion on pressing issues.
- Establish citizen-led commissions overseen by secretaries and special advisors for policy development.
- Create standards for a new national census that takes into account current needs. (DOC)
- Experiment with the debate format to allow for different ways of engagement from political figures. (DOS, DPA)

## Intergovernmental Relations

- Encourage interdepartmental collaboration with dedicated channels of communication and project directives.
- Improve standards for the audit process while maintaining auditor autonomy. (DOS)
- Engage in public, regular conversations with members of Congress regarding legislation to work towards consensus.

## Foreign Relations

- Encourage collaboration and work to improve relations with all foreign nations to achieve common goals in the city roleplay community. (DOS)
- Re-establish the Ambassador to the United Nations role to represent Redmont in the United Nations and during multinational disputes. (DOS)
- Work closely with the members of the United Nations to develop the infrastructure for future international sporting events. (DOS, DPA)

### Department Spotlight: State (DOS)

The Department of State will continue to play a critical, independent role under our administration, with employees continuing to have autonomy in performing the duties of their jobs. We will have the department implement some formal standards that take that flexibility into account and work to strengthen its relationship with the four towns and with our foreign allies.

# Town Activity

- Check in regularly with towns to determine current needs and rates of progress. Have auditors assist in preparing truncated monthly reports to evaluate current town activity and methods of recourse. (DOS)
- Review grant system and eliminate redundant grants. Establish new grants for town infrastructure. (DOS)
- Encourage voluntary, increased town/federal government collaboration while also maintaining a good level of autonomy for towns and their governments. (DOS)
- Restock and stabilize town clinics and animal shelters. Ensure they and other town infrastructure buildings are properly managed. (DOS, DOH, DOI, DOJ, DPA)
- Ensure events are held in each town on a regular basis and that each town has fully-developed event infrastructure. (DOS, DPA)

# Support our new players and new businesses.

New players and businessowners currently face extraordinary challenges starting out that we know we have it in us to correct. For the sake of our nation's future, we must make it easier for those starting out to claim the opportunities at success that our nation provides.

## The Learning Curve

- Task the Department of Education with developing macro-level strategies for lessening the learning curve for new players, involving a three-stage process of outreach (identifying areas of need), implementation (determining and enacting ways to improve education), and evaluation (seeing what works and what doesn't). (DOE)
- Explore the possibility of making the tour guide profession a licensed job. (DOE)
- Encourage flexibility for guides in conducting tours that focuses on primarily what the player is looking to get out of the server. (DOE)
- Establish a program for guides to follow up with and forge stronger bonds with the players they give tours to in their early days, with players' permission. (DOE)
- Have guides consistently solicit feedback to re-evaluate their regular tour structures to match the changing needs of the new player population. (DOE)

## Early Guidance and Support

- Look into developing more specific grants for new players and businesses. (DOC)
- Develop guides that focus solely on providing overviews of the nation's main industries and fields. (DOE, DOC)
- Conduct regular outreach with new players to determine their needs for future reference. (DOE)
- Connect new players with helpful services through the tour guide program, making use of new portals and information programs. (DOE)

### Department Spotlight: Education (DOE)

The Department of Education under our administration will play a critical, outsized role in furthering our goals to support new players and improve education and accessibility for all with its employment of guides, professors, and archivists. We plan to grow every division of this department to match the growing needs of the player base for guidance and educational resources.

## Political Engagement

- Encourage new players to apply for government jobs and citizen-led commissions. (DOE, DPA)
- Establish a Government House internship program for players looking to get involved in government.
- Create new, detailed how-to guides that outline the government system for players wanting to get involved. (DOE, DOS)
- Develop a program that seeks to inform newer players of ways they can participate and get involved in their government. (DOE, DOS)  

# Improve education and accessibility nationwide.

The federal government provides a wide range of programs aimed at supporting players of all kinds and experience levels, but it must do a better job at letting players know they exist. Beyond that, we see much room for improvement in player education and accessibility initiatives to provide a better, well-rounded understanding of the way things work and how to get around. We also see public affairs events and media as major methods of engagement for citizens.

## Information and Constituent Services

- Establish a new constituent services portal and directory on several platforms including Discord and Forums to direct players to the correct office for their specific needs.
- Make better use of new technology and automation in the Government House for better management of tasks.
- Encourage all departments to utilize their Discord servers more effectively.
- Revive the Archivist program with a new focus on covering historical events, rather than individual companies or entities. (DOC)
- Foster regular collaboration between the Departments of Public Affairs and Education and others to develop public service announcements. (DPA, DOE)

## Field Education and Support

- Foster a partnership between the Departments of Legal Affairs and Education for the purpose of developing legal education programs. (DLA, DOE)
- Implement new Archivist commission programs to support the journalism industry. (DOE)
- Conduct regular outreach efforts between prominent members of the nation's many industries and the Department of Education to determine areas of need. (DOC, DOE)

### Department Spotlight: Legal Affairs (DLA)

Our main goal for the Department of Legal Affairs is to restore activity and hire more employees, as for the past several weeks, the department has struggled with maintaining its case load. Aside from that, we plan on using this department as a resource for all lawyers.  

## Wayfinding

- Work with the Departments of Construction and Transport, Education, and Public Affairs to improve wayfinding and accessibility through new maps and destination signage throughout the city. (DCT, DOE, DPA)
- Add more markers to the Dynmap to reflect future landmarks and public spaces.

## Public Affairs

- Host at least one event per week. We mean it this time. (DPA)
- Revamp Redmont's social media accounts by creating a regular content stream and making use of scheduled posts. Develop strategies for better social media engagement. (DPA)
- Host regular photo competitions for the public, including of random events throughout the term. (DPA)
- Feature more non-DPA content for social media (e.g. photos from other DC content creators). (DPA)
- Create a comprehensive, searchable photo archive using player photos, organized into folders. (DPA)
- Provide increased autonomy for event managers to allow use of government resources and methods of communication. (DPA)
- Conduct more debate-filled political events even where there isn’t an election. (DPA, DOS)
- Work with the Department of State to facilitate further inter-town collaboration for events. (DPA, DOS)
- Expand DCTV to become a full-scale organization within the Department of Public Affairs and work on adapting content to multiple platforms to maximize overall outreach efforts. (DPA)

### Department Spotlight: Public Affairs (DPA)

The Department of Public Affairs is currently working to return to full activity levels after several slow months. Under our administration, we plan on restoring the department to its former glory by improving the consistency of events and social media posts, continuing to experiment with new methods of doing things, and growing the DCTV program.

# Continue to develop neighborhoods and culture.

Several months after our move to the new capital, we see much potential in the many communities that have developed in Reveille thus far. However, we know there remains much work to be done to develop neighborhood character and improve mobility and aesthetics throughout the city. We will work to establish a unified, yet flexible urban planning and community development strategy to future-proof our neighborhoods.

## Urban Planning

- Build up, then out. Support increasing density in urban cores to combat urban sprawl. (DCT)
- Create transit-oriented development project grants. Support the development of dense areas around transit stations to make streetscapes more walkable. (DCT, DOC)
- Develop incentives for mixed-use development on commercial and skyscraper plots. (DCT, DOC)
- Convert some empty, unused government-owned plots into spaces for public use. Encourage the construction of new public space throughout the city. (DCT, DOI)

### Department Spotlight: Construction and Transport (DCT)

The Department of Construction and Transport under our administration will place a greater focus on urban planning, community development, and mobility, in addition to its existing duties. We will consider the creation of a new 'Planner' occupation for urban and transit planning and community development. Existing DCT groups will remain unaffected.

## Community Development

- Work with the public to come up with ways to improve neighborhood character, including through street naming programs. (DCT, DPA)
- Develop ways to improve and change the aesthetic of themed neighborhoods within Reveille to encourage flexibility within reason. (DCT)
- Task the Department of Construction & Department with developing strategies for the continued differentiation and growth of themed neighborhoods. (DCT)

## Transportation and Mobility

- Lower the cost of all public transportation. (DCT, DOC)
- Bring public transportation options to all towns and encourage the construction of multimodal hubs. (DCT, DOS)

# Keep our citizens and communities safe and healthy.

Citizens have a right to be safe in their own communities, whether that is from fellow citizens or from ill health. Our plans for community safety involve initiatives to expand and improve our police force, reform our healthcare system, and keep our environment clean.

## Public Safety

- Expand the police force to address community needs. (DOJ)
- Explore the possibility for recruits to become an exam job as the first part of a multi-stage hiring process. (DOJ)
- Establish a new structure for criminal investigations. (DOJ)
- Host more training sessions, drills, and simulations for police officers to improve department collaboration and morale. (DOJ)
- Continue to build upon existing training methods for officers and create a new training checklist. (DOJ)
- Implement enhanced, common-sense knowledge tests, spot checks, check-ins, and interviews for officers to improve officer training and keep officers accountable. (DOJ, DOE)

### Department Spotlight: Justice (DOJ)

The Department of Justice under our administration will build upon the successful transparency and training initiatives of the current administration to effectively further grow the police force. It will work to improve accountability for officers while determining ways to expand upon criminal roleplay.

## Criminal Roleplay

- Create opportunities for criminal roleplay using NPCs in underutilized yet common areas of the city, including parks and train stations. (DOJ)
- Add more interactive features to the Redmont prison. (DOJ, DCT)
- Find ways to allow criminal organizations to co-exist with the police force in an equal, healthy, and competitive way. (DOJ)

## Physical Health

- Establish a joint /911 GUI system that incorporates both the current /emt and /complaint systems. (DOJ, DOH)
- Promote and encourage the use of the EMT program through events and other large-scale marketing strategies. (DOH, DOE, DPA)
- Explore ways to improve the training process and guides for doctors and EMTs. (DOH, DOE)
- Work with local businesses to source supplies for the hospital system. (DOH)
- Ask staff to tweak the rates of contracting diseases and their severity levels. (DOH)
- Improve the infraction management system to ensure doctors are properly held accountable for critical errors. (DOH)
- Establish a new, low-maintenance community clinic system with a more limited range of cures for underserved areas of the city. (DOH, DCT)

### Department Spotlight: Health (DOH)

The Department of Health will work to develop new ways to keep citizens healthy in an efficient manner under our administration, primarily through improved healthcare coverage and better training, material sourcing, and accountability.

## Environmental Health

- Commission the development of new pocket parks and improved green space throughout the city from the Departments of Construction and Transport and the Interior. (DOI, DCT)
- Encourage further collaboration between the Departments of Construction and Transport and the Interior for mutual support on the construction of new national wonders and monuments. (DOI, DCT)
- Improve transparency on the supply side. (DOI)
- Hire more employees at the Department of the Interior in order to ensure the wilderness remains properly maintained. (DOI)

### Department Spotlight: Interior (DOI)

The Department of the Interior under our administration will work to improve activity on all fronts and support and actively facilitate the development of improved green space throughout the city and the construction of new national wonders and monuments.

# Encourage government fiscal responsibility.

Taxpayer money should always go to causes that serve to benefit all of us, and not just a select few. We will always take into account the needs of our communities as we determine our monthly budgets and work to eliminate unnecessary spending over time.

## Economy

- Use only hard data and statistics regarding economic and trends to guide any decisions regarding changes to government economic and fiscal policy. (DOC)
- Maintain a healthy balance between the public and private job markets.

## Government Budgets

- Be more precise with publicized, itemized government budgets. Include more visual elements to assist citizens in understanding where their money is going. (DOC, DOE)
- Launch a formal review at the start of the term to determine areas where we can make budget cuts without affecting important programs. (DOC)

## Taxation

- Review current income and corporate tax rates to ensure they are responsible, fair, and low for the average Redmont citizen.
- Crack down on tax evasion, money laundering, and other economic white-collar crimes through investigating loopholes and determining areas where they may exist. (DOC, DLA)

### Department Spotlight: Commerce (DOC)

The Department of Commerce under our administration will play a stable role in collecting economic data and statistics for purposes of developing fiscal and economic policy. We plan on making better use of automation and improved training processes to maintain activity and fill voids in leadership in this department.

## Regulation

- Beyond existing regulations, we do not see a need to add to current restrictions on business in Redmont.
- Task the Departments of Commerce and Education with helping simplify and limit the burden of business regulations on new businesses. (DOC)

*All policies and goals listed here are subject to change and evolve over time (including during the campaign) to reflect current needs and in response to player feedback.*
